Weight tying reduces trainable parameters by reusing the embedding matrix for output logits. It can also provide regularization by connecting input and output token representations.

Follow-up

How many parameters does weight tying save?

For a vocabulary of V tokens and hidden size d, tying avoids a separate V × d output projection matrix (assuming no separate untied head).

05 · Small details that matter

Spacing: 8px system; 24–32px between major document sections.

Borders: Quiet 1px separators; avoid heavy shadows and stacked cards.

Corner radii: 6px source chips, 8px controls, 12px panels.

Icons: Consistent 16px iconography, approximately 1.75px stroke.

Motion: Restrained 120–180ms transitions; honor reduced-motion preferences.

Accessibility: Clear focus states, readable contrast, and labels that do not rely on color alone.
