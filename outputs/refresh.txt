refresh
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What the refresh did, from the store saved after it and the plan taken
before it (never the agent's prose). Refresh start = the first refresh
run's start. New claims: ids the plan does not list, less any already
retired before the refresh (the plan skips retired claims). Retired by
refresh apply: classed retired by the plan. Retired by the re-map: a
retired_at in the refresh, not a repair's retire. Verdicts: each claim's
saved check; a repair at round r in the refresh means not supported after
round r-1.
